Reflections

One of the most valuable lessons from this project was learning that effective social media management goes beyond simply publishing content. Consistency, organization, and proper planning play an important role in maintaining a reliable online presence. Scheduling content in advance helped create a smoother workflow, while keeping posts and platforms organized made it easier to manage daily social media activities. Using tools such as Buffer and Meta Business Suite also showed me how the right systems can make content management more efficient and consistent.
